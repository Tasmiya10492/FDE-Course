# Client Discovery Call Preparation

## Objective

Understand the current HR leave management process,
identify challenges and discover opportunities for improvement.

## Three Hypotheses

1. Leave approvals may be delayed because managers
   do not review requests promptly.

2. Employees may lack visibility into their leave
   request status and pending approvals.

3. Leave policies may be difficult to understand
   across different employee categories.

## Three Discovery Questions

1. Can you explain the complete leave request process
   from submission to approval or rejection?

2. What are the most common problems employees and
   managers face during the leave approval process?

3. What information or functionality would help
   employees, managers and HR track leave requests?

## Information to Collect

- Current leave request workflow.
- Employee and manager responsibilities.
- Approval turnaround time.
- Common reasons for rejection.
- Policy differences between employee categories.
- Current reporting and tracking methods.

## Expected Outcome

Understand the client's actual requirements,
validate or reject the hypotheses and identify
the next steps before recommending a technical solution.
