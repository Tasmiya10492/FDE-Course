# Disciplinary records (Policy)

Purpose: this policy explains the rule on disciplinary records.

Rule: Disciplinary records are kept for 12 months and seen only by HR.

Scope: it applies to everyone who works with the hr leave service.

Exceptions: an exception needs approval from the HR administrator.
