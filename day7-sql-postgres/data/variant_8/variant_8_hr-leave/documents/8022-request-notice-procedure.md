# Request notice (Procedure)

Follow these steps.

1. Check who is asking and that they may see this information.
2. Apply the rule: Leave of more than 5 days must be requested 4 weeks ahead.
3. Write down what you decided and why.
4. If the case is unusual, ask the HR administrator.
