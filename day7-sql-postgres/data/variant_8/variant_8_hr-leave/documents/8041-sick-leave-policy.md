# Sick leave (Policy)

Purpose: this policy explains the rule on sick leave.

Rule: An employee must tell their line manager before 10:00 on the first day of sickness.

Scope: it applies to everyone who works with the hr leave service.

Exceptions: an exception needs approval from the HR administrator.
