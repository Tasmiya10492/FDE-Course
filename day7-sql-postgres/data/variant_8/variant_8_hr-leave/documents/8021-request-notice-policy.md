# Request notice (Policy)

Purpose: this policy explains the rule on request notice.

Rule: Leave of more than 5 days must be requested 4 weeks ahead.

Scope: it applies to everyone who works with the hr leave service.

Exceptions: an exception needs approval from the HR administrator.
