# Parental leave (Policy)

Purpose: this policy explains the rule on parental leave.

Rule: Parental leave is up to 26 weeks with 4 weeks' notice.

Scope: it applies to everyone who works with the hr leave service.

Exceptions: an exception needs approval from the HR administrator.
