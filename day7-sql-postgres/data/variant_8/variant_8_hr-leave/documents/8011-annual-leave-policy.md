# Annual leave (Policy)

Purpose: this policy explains the rule on annual leave.

Rule: A full-time employee receives 25 days of paid annual leave a year.

Scope: it applies to everyone who works with the hr leave service.

Exceptions: an exception needs approval from the HR administrator.
