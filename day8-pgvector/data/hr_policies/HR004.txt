Title: Leave Approval Process

This is a synthetic HR policy document created for the Day 6 RAG learning project.

Leave requests are reviewed by the employee's reporting manager.

The manager checks team availability, workload, and business requirements before making a decision.

Approved requests are updated in the HR portal.

If a request is rejected, the manager should provide a reason to the employee.

Employees can contact HR if an approval is delayed.
