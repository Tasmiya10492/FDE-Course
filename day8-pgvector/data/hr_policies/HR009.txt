Title: Maternity Leave

This is a synthetic HR policy document created for the Day 6 RAG learning project.

Eligible employees may apply for maternity leave according to applicable law and company policy.

Employees should contact HR to understand eligibility, required documentation, and applicable leave duration.

The employee should coordinate with HR and the reporting manager before the planned leave start date whenever possible.

HR is responsible for explaining the applicable process and required documents.
