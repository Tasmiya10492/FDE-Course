Title: Leave Balance Calculation

This is a synthetic HR policy document created for the Day 6 RAG learning project.

An employee's leave balance represents the eligible leave available for use.

The balance may depend on leave entitlement, leave already taken, approved future leave, and applicable carry-forward rules.

Employees should use the HR portal to view their current leave balance.

If the displayed balance appears incorrect, employees should contact HR for verification.
