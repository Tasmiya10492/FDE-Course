Title: Sick Leave Eligibility

This is a synthetic HR policy document created for the Day 6 RAG learning project.

Employees who are unable to work due to illness may apply for sick leave according to their employment terms and company policy.

Employees should inform their reporting manager as soon as possible when they are unable to attend work.

Sick leave eligibility and available days depend on the employee's applicable leave policy.

Employees can contact HR to confirm their sick leave balance.
