Title: Annual Leave Entitlement

This is a synthetic HR policy document created for the Day 6 RAG learning project.

Employees receive an annual leave entitlement based on their employment terms and company policy.

The total number of annual leave days may vary depending on employee grade, contract type, and length of service.

Employees can check their available leave balance through the HR portal.

For entitlement-related questions, employees should contact the HR department.
