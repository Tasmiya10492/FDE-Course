Title: Paternity Leave

This is a synthetic HR policy document created for the Day 6 RAG learning project.

Eligible employees may apply for paternity leave according to applicable law and company policy.

Employees should contact HR to confirm eligibility and the available leave entitlement.

A leave request should be submitted through the HR portal with the expected dates.

The reporting manager reviews the request, and HR can assist with policy-related questions.
