Title: Unpaid Leave

This is a synthetic HR policy document created for the Day 6 RAG learning project.

Employees may request unpaid leave when paid leave balances are insufficient or when the situation requires an extended absence.

Unpaid leave requests are subject to management approval and company policy.

Employees should discuss the proposed dates with their reporting manager before submitting the request.

HR can explain how approved unpaid leave may affect salary and other employment benefits.
