Title: Unauthorized Absence

This is a synthetic HR policy document created for the Day 6 RAG learning project.

An absence may be considered unauthorized when an employee does not attend work without approved leave or appropriate notification.

Employees should inform their reporting manager as soon as possible if an unexpected situation prevents them from attending work.

Managers should record and review unexplained absences according to company policy.

Employees should contact HR to resolve attendance records or absence-related concerns.
