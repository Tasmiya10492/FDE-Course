Title: Cancelling Leave Requests

This is a synthetic HR policy document created for the Day 6 RAG learning project.

Employees who no longer need approved leave should submit a cancellation request through the HR portal.

The reporting manager must review the cancellation request.

The employee should check the HR portal to confirm that the leave cancellation has been approved.

If the cancellation option is unavailable, the employee should contact HR for assistance.
