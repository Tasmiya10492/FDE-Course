Title: Leave During Notice Period

This is a synthetic HR policy document created for the Day 6 RAG learning project.

Employees serving a notice period should follow the normal leave request process unless company policy specifies otherwise.

Leave requests during the notice period require review by the reporting manager and HR where applicable.

Approval depends on business requirements, employment terms, and company policy.

Employees should contact HR if they need clarification about unused leave or final settlement.
