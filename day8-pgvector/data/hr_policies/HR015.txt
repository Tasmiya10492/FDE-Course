Title: Half-Day Leave

This is a synthetic HR policy document created for the Day 6 RAG learning project.

Employees may request half-day leave when they need to be absent for part of a working day.

The employee should select the appropriate half-day option in the HR portal.

The request must be reviewed by the reporting manager.

If the half-day option is unavailable or the employee is unsure which option to select, HR can provide assistance.
