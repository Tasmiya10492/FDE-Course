Title: Applying for Annual Leave

This is a synthetic HR policy document created for the Day 6 RAG learning project.

Employees must submit annual leave requests through the official HR portal.

Each request should include the leave start date, end date, and reason for leave.

Employees are encouraged to submit requests in advance to allow sufficient time for review.

Submitting a request does not guarantee approval. Employees should wait for manager approval before taking leave.
