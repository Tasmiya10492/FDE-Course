Title: Leave Escalation and Disputes

This is a synthetic HR policy document created for the Day 6 RAG learning project.

Employees who disagree with a leave decision should first discuss the matter with their reporting manager.

If the issue remains unresolved, employees may contact the HR department for review.

HR should examine the request details, relevant policy, and any supporting information.

Sensitive leave disputes should be handled confidentially through the appropriate HR process.
