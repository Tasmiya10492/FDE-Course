Title: Annual Leave Eligibility

This is a synthetic HR policy document created
for the Day 6 RAG learning project.

Employees who have completed the required
eligibility period are entitled to apply for
annual leave.

New employees must check their employment terms
and HR policy before applying.

All annual leave requests must be submitted
through the official HR portal.

The HR manager is responsible for reviewing
and approving requests.
