Title: Leave Carry Forward

This is a synthetic HR policy document created for the Day 6 RAG learning project.

Annual leave carry forward is subject to the company's leave policy and applicable employment terms.

The maximum number of days that can be carried forward may vary according to employee eligibility and company rules.

Employees should review their leave balance before the end of the leave year.

HR can confirm the applicable carry-forward limit and expiry date.
