# Consulting Practice: Make Our Support Team Faster — Workplan

## Objective
Identify the main causes of delays in customer support and develop a prioritised improvement plan while maintaining customer satisfaction and resolution quality.

## Approach
The investigation follows the four branches of the Day 11 MECE issue tree:

1. Waiting before an agent starts handling a request.
2. Time spent actively handling requests.
3. Time waiting for information or assistance.
4. Time taken to close a resolved request.

## Workplan
The workplan includes 13 ordered tasks covering problem definition, data collection, data validation, analysis of all four branches, bottleneck prioritisation, POC design, success criteria and stakeholder recommendations.

Each task has an assigned owner, identified risk, best/likely/worst estimate and an assumption.

## Estimated effort
- Best case: 19 hours
- Likely case: 36 hours
- Worst case: 60 hours

These are illustrative person-hour estimates. Some investigation activities can run in parallel.

## Key risks
- Incomplete or inaccurate ticket data.
- Missing workflow timestamps.
- Limited support-agent availability.
- Incorrect prioritisation of root causes.
- Improvement in speed negatively affecting customer satisfaction or resolution quality.

## Recommendation
Measure the time spent in all four workflow stages before selecting a solution. Prioritise the largest evidence-based bottleneck, develop a POC and measure its impact against the existing process. Advance to Pilot only when the POC demonstrates measurable improvement without compromising service quality.
