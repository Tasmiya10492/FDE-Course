# Day 18 — Dana Consulting Rehearsal

## Objective

Agree on a safe path to the Knowledge Assistant launch without
compromising employee data, access control or service reliability.

## Opening

"Dana, I agree with the goal of getting the Knowledge Assistant
ready for the business. I want to understand what makes Friday
important so we can choose a safe delivery plan."

## Questions to ask

1. Why is Friday the requested launch date?
2. Who made the decision, and who owns the deadline?
3. Is Friday a contractual deadline, a customer commitment or a target?
4. What is the business impact if the date moves?
5. What is the main concern behind the request for a shared admin password?
6. Who currently owns the administrative credentials?

## Two clear boundaries

- I cannot approve a shared administrator password. Each person
  needs an individual account, least-privilege access and an audit trail.
- I cannot recommend an unrestricted production release while
  High-severity findings remain unresolved without documented
  risk-owner acceptance and appropriate mitigations.

## Two delivery options

Option A — Limited pilot on Friday, 27 November 2026:
- Restrict access to an approved pilot group.
- Use individual accounts and controlled permissions.
- Complete security triage and required mitigations.
- Obtain explicit approval from the accountable risk owner.
- Monitor errors, access and availability.

Option B — Full release on Monday, 30 November 2026:
- Remediate the High findings where fixes are available.
- Rebuild and rescan the images.
- Validate access control and operational readiness.
- Obtain release and risk-owner sign-off.

Dates are proposed targets, not promises of completed remediation.

## Close

"Can we agree on the pilot or full-release path, name the
decision owner, and document the remaining actions and dates?"
